这是渭上公寓的内部大厅。进门就是刷脸通行的闸机，墙上“一站式服务社区”的标语格外醒目。前台写着暖心的“欢迎回家”，宿管阿姨在这里守护我们的日常。走廊干净明亮，公告栏清晰张贴公寓安全须知与各类服务联系方式，随处可见完整的消防设施。大厅还配备了直饮水机、无人售卖冰柜，生活设施齐全。处处细致的设置，守护着我们在校舒适安心的住宿生活。
This is the inner hall of Weishang Apartment. At the entrance are face‑recognition turnstiles, and the slogan "one‑stop service community" on the wall stands out. The warm sign "Welcome home" is displayed at the front desk, where dorm supervisors take care of our daily life. The corridor is clean and bright. The notice board posts dorm safety rules and service contacts, and complete fire‑fighting equipment can be seen everywhere. There are water dispensers and unmanned vending freezers for our convenience. All these thoughtful facilities create a safe and comfortable living space for us students.
